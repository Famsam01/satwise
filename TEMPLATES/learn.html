About Satwise

Satwise is a Bitcoin-only investing app. You set a goal, choose a plan and stack Bitcoin steadily over time, so you don't have to guess the perfect day to buy.

How it works

  1. 1
    Create your account

    Sign up with a username, email and phone number. An invitation code is optional.

  2. 2
    Set a goal

    Choose how much Bitcoin you want to stack, counted in sats. One Bitcoin is 100,000,000 sats.

  3. 3
    Pick a plan

    Standard, Premium or Advanced. Plans change your fees and features, not your risk.

  4. 4
    Buy Bitcoin

    Buy whenever you like with Buy now, or let your plan buy for you on a schedule.

  5. 5
    Track and withdraw

    Watch your balance and goal on the Home page, and withdraw when you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Will I make a profit?

We can't promise that. Bitcoin's price goes up and down, so your stack can be worth more or less than you put in. Plans change fees and features, not returns.

What is a sat?

A sat (satoshi) is the smallest piece of Bitcoin. 1 Bitcoin equals 100,000,000 sats, so you can start with a tiny amount and still see progress.

How much do I need to start?

It depends on the plan. Standard starts at $50 a month, Premium at $250 and Advanced at $1,000. You can see all three on the Goals page.

What are the fees?

Each plan charges a fee on every buy: 1% on Standard, 0.6% on Premium and 0.35% on Advanced. The plan simulator shows exactly how much you'd pay.

How do referrals work?

Share your link. When a friend signs up with your code, makes a first buy and passes a short waiting period, you both get bonus sats. It's one level only, so you never earn from your friend's friends.

What is a Stack Circle?

A group of up to 5 people saving together. Everyone keeps their own Bitcoin. If all members buy in the same week, the circle's streak grows and it earns a fee discount.

How do withdrawals work?

Use the Withdraw button on the Home page. Network fees and timing can vary, and we show them before you confirm.

Why does the chart say "Simulated"?

That means live prices couldn't be loaded, for example when you're offline. The chart then shows sample data so the page still works.
